# Brief — Ulpan Ivrit

Public site for Ulpan Ivrit in Germany. Operator: [BiFoDe e.V.](https://www.bifode.org). Live URL: https://www.ulpan-ivrit.de

## Audience

Hebrew learners in Germany (and their Gemeinden / Träger) who need a clear path: membership → online Moodle and/or Präsenz group.

## Job of the site

Convert a visitor into a membership enquiry or a location request. It is a vitrine, not a campus: no accounts, no Moodle login, no payment.

## Product facts

- WZO / Ofek Israeli / Sochnut project, delivered in Germany by BiFoDe e.V.
- Methodology: Hebrew University of Jerusalem.
- Online: individual Moodle at https://ulpan.bifode.org after admission.
- Vor Ort: groups hosted by Gemeinde, Verein, or Community; typical session two full hours, weekly, spoken Hebrew.
- Enrolment: https://www.bifode.org/mitglied-werden
- Contact: info@bifode.org

## Locales

German is canonical (no URL prefix). Russian and English are `/ru` and `/en`. Hebrew is configured as a fallback to German only.

## Out of scope

Other BiFoDe properties and other repos. This tree is only www.ulpan-ivrit.de.
