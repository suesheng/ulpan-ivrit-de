# Ulpan Ivrit — www.ulpan-ivrit.de

WZO / Ofek Israeli / Sochnut project, delivered in Germany by [BiFoDe e.V.](https://www.bifode.org). Visual design is produced in Open Design, then implemented here as Astro and deployed on Vercel.

Membership in the Verein unlocks Präsenz and online classes, lectures, and event announcements. Typical Präsenz session: two full hours, weekly, with a break; spoken Hebrew; materials mirrored in Moodle. Methodology: Hebrew University of Jerusalem. Teachers in community projects: recruited and co-financed when possible, plus Hishtalmut (teacher professional development) by HUJI lecturers.

- **Online:** individual Moodle at [ulpan.bifode.org](https://ulpan.bifode.org)
- **Vor Ort:** groups hosted by Gemeinde, Verein, or Community — contact form at `/vor-ort`
- **Enrolment:** [Mitglied werden](https://www.bifode.org/mitglied-werden)
- **Repo / deploy:** [github.com/suesheng/ulpan-ivrit-de](https://github.com/suesheng/ulpan-ivrit-de) → Vercel (`master`)

Locales: German (default, no prefix), Russian (`/ru`), English (`/en`). Legal pages live on this domain (`/impressum`, `/datenschutz`, `/cookies`).
